Before you start

Choose the owner name and currency carefully. Amounts are displayed using that currency's symbol and digit grouping throughout the app.

First setup

From empty app to first rent record.

1

Set owner details

Enter the owner full name and select the currency you use for rent collection.

2

Add a tenancy

Add the property, tenant, rent amount, deposit, start date, and optional tenant email address.

3

Attach documents

Add up to five files, including ID proof, address proof, deed, and two other documents.

4

Record payments

Payments fill completed rent periods in date order, keeping the ledger predictable.

Payments and discounts

A discount must be for one rent period and cannot exceed that period's rent. Rent Manager does not add extra-charge adjustments, so the ledger stays focused on rent, discounts, and payments.

Receipts

Issue a receipt only when the period is fully paid. The receipt PDF includes the tenant, rent period, paid amount, and owner name. After a receipt is issued, that period is frozen.

Email actions

Contract, statement, and reminder actions open your email app with a prepared draft. If the tenant has an email address, it is filled in. You review and send the message yourself.

Maintenance and tax logs

Log maintenance or tax activity against a tenancy for reference. These amounts do not change rent due, discounts, payments, or receipt totals.

Pro features

More tenancies and backup.

The first tenancy is free. Adding another tenancy, Google Drive backup, and automatic daily backup require the monthly Pro subscription.
