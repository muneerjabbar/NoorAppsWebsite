Privacy policy

Your records stay in your control.

Rent Manager is built around local records on your device, with optional backup features when you choose to use them.

Last updated: 15 October 2026 No Rent Manager account required

Contact

Questions about privacy or data handling can be sent to noorappssupport@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Rent Manager by Noor Apps stores tenancy records, payments, discounts, receipts, maintenance logs, and optional documents on your device.

No Rent Manager account

The app does not require a Rent Manager account. You can use core features without creating a Noor Apps login.

Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is available with Pro and stores a backup in a RentManager folder in the Google account you choose. Restoring a backup replaces the data on the device.

Sharing and email drafts

Sharing a contract, statement, reminder, or receipt opens the app you choose. Email actions create a draft only; Rent Manager does not send email itself.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by app stores.

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the App Store. Manage subscription status, billing, cancellation, and refunds through the store account used to subscribe.

Website

The official Rent Manager website is http://rentmanager.noorapps.net/.
