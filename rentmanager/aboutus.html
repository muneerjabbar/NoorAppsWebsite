Why Rent Manager?

Rental work often lives across notebooks, messages, PDFs, and spreadsheets. Rent Manager brings the core records into one clear app.

A

Useful before clever

Rent Manager focuses on the daily jobs owners need: tenants, periods, payments, receipts, reminders, and maintenance notes.

B

Local-first records

The app is designed around device-held data, with optional Google Drive backup available for Pro users.

+

Designed to grow

Start with one tenancy for free, then upgrade when you need extra tenancies and backup automation.

Our promise

Make rental records easier to trust.

The app uses clear rules like issuing receipts only after full payment and freezing receipted periods, so records stay consistent over time.
