About Noor Apps

Technology that benefits communities.

At Noor Apps, we believe technology should make a meaningful difference in people’s lives. We build applications that solve real-world problems for individuals, families, and communities — practical tools for ordinary days, not complicated enterprise software.

Some of our projects grow as community initiatives. Others are commercial products that sustain the work and let us keep making useful apps. Across all of them, the same idea holds: records should be easy to trust, and your information should stay in your hands. We are committed to reliable, user-friendly applications built with sincerity, quality, and purpose.

Useful before clever

Each app focuses on the daily jobs people actually do — tasks, rent, calls, receipts, reminders — and leaves the rest out.

Local-first privacy

No account is required to start. Plans, tenancies, and lists live on the phone. Backup, when it exists, is optional and yours.

Free to begin

Core features are useful without a subscription. Pro unlocks higher limits and extras inside the same app when you need them.
